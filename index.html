Recommendations

What Should Follow

The analyses above describe what state literacy laws contain. This companion sets out what we think should follow — twenty-seven recommendations drawn from the analysis and from the research literature, each tied to the policy lever it depends on: law, guidance, implementation or practice. Where an indicator in the legislation analysis speaks to a recommendation, the crosswalk names it and says plainly what the indicator can and cannot establish.

Recommendations · 6 themes

Literacy Policy Recommendations

Twenty-seven recommendations across six themes — a more expansive view of reading, alignment with the research, teacher supports, student supports, home–school collaboration, and coherence. Filter by policy lever, open the crosswalk to the legislation analysis, or follow a marker to the relevant passage of the AFT resolution.
